## panel-push: dramatisation, no endorsement

`skills/panel-push/SKILL.md` has the agent dramatise named software developers
in character, drawing on their public writing, talks and known positions. Every
line it produces is invented for the session. Nothing it says is a quote, and
none of the people it names endorse, review or are affiliated with this project
or with Sage.is.

## presentation-interview: a named method, no endorsement

`skills/presentation-interview/SKILL.md` has the agent run its interview in
the manner of Dorie Clark, drawing on her published method and public
positions on strategic communication. The questions and lines it produces are
invented for the session. She does not endorse, review or have any
affiliation with this skill, this project or Sage.is.

## todo-scope: mirrors Startr/TodoScope

`skills/todo-scope/SKILL.md` restates the TODO.md convention that
[Startr/TodoScope](https://github.com/Startr/TodoScope) reads. TodoScope's
`kanban.py` is the source of truth and the canonical copy of the skill lives in
that repository; this copy follows it.
